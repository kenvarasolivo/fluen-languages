# Topic illustration set

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The existing `public/illustrations/fluen-shape-friends.png` was the style and identity reference. Six topic scenes use one or two characters; the conversation image reunites the original three with microphones. Optimized WebP assets retain transparency and live in `public/illustrations/`.

The topic scene appears in a compact banner above the writing card. The entire banner opens a full-screen picker with six illustrated topic cards and progress. Picking a card closes the dialog and updates the artwork and exercise. Escape or the close button dismisses it. Conversation keeps its topic selector and scene in settings. The microphone trio appears on the conversation welcome screen.

## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: Fluen language-learning app topic illustration. Input image is a style reference, not a required group composition. Minimal flat 2D playful brand editorial illustration. Rounded geometric mascot shapes, tiny navy dot eyes and curved smiling mouths, solid color fills, crisp softly rounded edges, no outlines, no gradients, no 3D or photorealism. Palette lavender #a39aff, butter yellow #ffe477, coral #ff786c, navy #132f62 with occasional blue or mint accent. Geometric bodies, optional tiny simple navy arms only where needed for carrying props. Wide airy landscape composition 3:2, centered, whole scene visible with generous margins, readable at small card size. Transparent alpha background, no ground rectangle or scenery backdrop. No letters, text, labels or watermarks. Use ONLY the character count specified below, no extra faces on objects.
